Recommended next steps

Protect your phone or computer

  1. Update your operating system, browser, and apps using their official update settings.
  2. Keep the built-in security protection enabled (for example, Windows Security, Google Play Protect, or Apple’s built-in protections).
  3. For a suspected infection, run a full scan with trusted security software obtained from its official source. Avoid “cleaner” downloads from pop-ups or unsolicited messages.
  4. Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication; do not share one-time passwords or install remote-access apps at a stranger’s request.
  5. If you are responding to an online financial cybercrime in India, contact your bank promptly and use the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in or helpline 1930. Share only information relevant to your incident.
